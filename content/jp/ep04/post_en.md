A renewal fee in a lease, the payment terms in a works contract, how far a guarantor's liability reaches — when the wording is unclear, people ask a lawyer, a licensed real-estate agent, or a consumer affairs centre. The usual order is: send a photo of the contract, then wonder afterwards how much of it the other person could see.

Pasting into an AI chat (episode 3) and showing a document to a professional are not the same situation. The adviser is a person, and that changes what may be hidden.

## What changes

| | Pasting into an AI | Asking a professional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counterparty's name | hide it | **sometimes keep it** (conflict check) |
| Counterparty's address, phone | hide | hide, as a rule |
| Account number, balance | hide | hide |
| Guarantor's name and contact | hide | keep only if the question is about them |
| Clause wording, amounts, dates | keep | keep |

A lawyer checks, before taking a case, whether the parties conflict with an existing client. If the counterparty's name is covered from the start, that check cannot be done, and the answer may turn out later to be that they cannot act. Some advisers prefer the name withheld in the first message and disclosed once the engagement is real. Asking which they prefer takes one line.

Account numbers, balances and a guarantor's phone number, on the other hand, are almost never used to interpret a contract. Those can be reduced whoever the adviser is.

## Three steps

1. Drop in the PDF or the photo.
2. Choose "purpose: sharing for advice". The counterparty's name, address and account become candidates; the clauses and the amounts stay.
3. Review what is being kept. If you need the counterparty's name, switch that one item back before you save.

The output is flattened to a picture, so the characters under a bar cannot be copied out. If you need to send text, replace proper nouns with placeholders such as "Lessor A".

## Why

This episode is about how an enquiry is handled, not about a legal requirement. Different advisers need different things, so when in doubt, ask in your first message how much you may withhold. If the document belongs to your employer, internal rules may restrict showing it to a third party at all.

Always check the recipient's own instructions.

Files are processed inside the browser and are not sent to a server.
