A mortgage application, a nursery place, a rental screening. A copy of the withholding slip comes up a few times a year. The usual order is: hand it over, then remember there may have been a My Number field on it.

The same document comes in forms that print the number and forms that do not. Which one is in your hand is not knowable until you look.

## Two forms

| Purpose | My Number printed | How it reaches you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| employee's copy | generally not | handed out at year end |
| filed with the tax office or municipality | some forms do | an employer may pass one on as your copy |

An employer may hand over the filing version as if it were your copy, and that one carries the number. Photocopy the sheet you were given and the number travels with it.

Whoever asked for the slip almost always wants the income figures. The amount paid, the figure after the employment income deduction and the withheld tax are usually enough; the My Number is not used for that.

## Three steps

1. Drop in the PDF or the photo.
2. Choose "purpose: submitting identity documents". The My Number becomes a candidate; the amounts and the name stay.
3. Download. The output is flattened to a picture, so nothing readable is left under a bar.

The payer's corporate number is printed too. Corporate numbers are published information, and a recipient may want it to confirm your employer. Whether to cover it follows their instruction.

## Why

Handling of the My Number is a separate scheme from the health insurance symbol and number (episode 1). Unless the recipient is in a position to carry out My Number related administration, there is usually no need to hand the number over. Dealings with your employer, the tax office or your municipality may not fall under that, and there are situations where the document should be submitted as it is.

Always check the recipient's own instructions.

Files are processed inside the browser and are not sent to a server.
