Japanese public-works tenders generally require masking of the "insured symbol/number" and the "insurer number" on copies of health-insurance qualification certificates. The basis is the disclosure restriction introduced by amendments to the Health Insurance Act, in force since 1 October 2020: asking for the symbol or number for purposes other than medical-insurance administration is restricted in principle.

Rejections almost always come from manual work. A copy covered with sticky notes or correction tape can show the digits through, depending on the scanner's light. A PDF with a black rectangle laid on top of the text may look redacted on screen while the text layer underneath is still selectable. And the branch number and the 2-D code are small enough on paper that they are the last things anyone notices.

## What to cover, and what to keep

| Document | Cover | Keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification certificate (employee / national / late-elderly insurance) | Insurer number, symbol, number, branch number, 2-D code | Name, date of birth, address, expiry, employer name |
| Standard remuneration decision notice | Insured reference number | Name, employer name, period |
| Residence record | My Number, residence code (when the recipient does not ask for them) | Name, address, issuer |

Review is done on the name, the date of birth and the employer name. Covering the numbers and accidentally covering the fields the reviewer needs are two different mistakes, and the second one also gets your submission sent back.

1. Drag in the PDF or the photo. Several files at once is fine.

3. Download. The output is flattened to an image, so no original text remains under the bars, and EXIF and annotations are stripped.

The 2-D code often carries the same information as the symbol and number. Redacting the digits and leaving the code defeats most of the point.

Masking requirements differ between contracting authorities, and some mask the copies themselves after receipt. **Always check the recipient's own instructions.** If they ask only for the symbol and number, there is no need to cover anything else.
