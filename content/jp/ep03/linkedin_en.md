That was my whole week. I'm building SafeFile, a browser-only tool that covers personal details in a document.

Pasting a contract into a chat to ask what a clause means is useful. I do it. But the page you paste also carries the other party's name, address, and bank account number. None of that helps answer the question.

My first instinct was to reuse the strictest setting. That was wrong, and it took a few tries to see why. Hide the amounts and the dates and the answer gets worse. The clause wording is what the model needs. So this mode keeps the words and the numbers that matter, and covers the people.

Two things I learned:

1. The goal is not "hide as much as possible". It's "keep the question answerable with less personal data in it". Those pull in opposite directions.

2. A black box over text is not enough. The output has to become a flat picture, or the hidden characters paste straight back out.

One caution I put in the article: your workplace rules may not allow a third-party chat at all. Read those first.
