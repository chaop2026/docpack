Settling a subsidy means attaching wage ledgers, employee registers, copies of bank books. The handbook may say "where necessary from the standpoint of protecting personal information, please apply masking as appropriate" — and that is where people stop, because the scope is not written down.

It is the same shape as a tender notice (episode 2). When the wording does not set the range, the judgement stays with whoever is submitting.

## Reading the wording

| How the handbook reads | Usually means | Easy to get wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mask as appropriate | no range given | covering the audited fields gets it returned |
| where a My Number appears, mask that part | the number only | it can sit in a margin of the wage ledger |
| submit a copy of the original | no processing | redacting first may be treated as a defect |
| keep the name | the name is audited | a date of birth may also be asked for, to tell two people apart |

"Cover more when unsure" is not the safe option. A subsidy audit checks who was paid, when, and how much — remove the amounts, the dates or the company name and that cannot be done. Covering too much is a defect; leaving a My Number in is a different problem. Both sides have limits.

For a bank book, sometimes only the lines relevant to the subsidy are needed. Even then the account number and the holder's name are used to confirm it, so hiding rows and hiding the account are two separate decisions.

## When you cannot tell

1. Search the grant rules and the handbook for "masking", "My Number", "copy"
2. Look at the same body's earlier handbooks — the wording is largely reused
3. Still unclear: ask the desk, and keep a record of the answer

## Why

METI's subsidy administration manual says "where necessary from the standpoint of protecting personal information, please apply masking as appropriate". No range is given. Other handbooks for particular programme types are specific, asking for a copy with the My Number field masked. The same scheme, different levels of detail.

Source: Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry, "Subsidy project administration manual" https://www.meti.go.jp/information_2/downloadfiles/2022_hojo_manual.pdf (Confirmed: 2026-11-01)
