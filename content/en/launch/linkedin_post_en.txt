I shipped a small tool for a very specific paperwork problem.

📄 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺
Companies bidding on public-works contracts in Japan must attach a copy of their health-insurance certificate — insurer/insured numbers blacked out, name and employer still visible. Miss one number → the bid comes back.

⚙️ 𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗶𝘁 𝗱𝗼𝗲𝘀
• Detects which document you uploaded (資格確認書, driver's licence, My Number card, 住民票)
• Applies the redaction rules for the stated purpose
• Flattens the output to an image — nothing remains under the black bars
• Everything runs in the browser; files never leave your machine

🧑‍💻 𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝗶𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗯𝘂𝗶𝗹𝘁
Solo developer, on top of slimfile.net (my file-compression service). Rails outside, plain JavaScript + Tesseract OCR inside. Free, no sign-up.

👉 Try it: https://slimfile.net/safe/jp/nyusatsu/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=launch

⚠️ Always follow the recipient's instructions — the tool applies rules; the rules come from them.
